# LabelLens — The food field guide

An original consumer product identity, informed by the clarity and generous
spacing described in VoltAgent's awesome-design-md collection (Airbnb and Wise).
References: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md,
https://getdesign.md/airbnb/design-md, https://getdesign.md/wise/design-md.
No brand assets, proprietary typefaces, or templates are copied.

## Character
An editorial food journal: useful, measured, tactile. Start with the customer's
next action, not a marketing hero. Photographs belong to real scanned products.
Do not fabricate products, ratings, testimonials, or live status indicators.

## Foundations
Paper #F7F4EC; ink #252A22; muted #62685D; tomato #B44028;
olive #43543A; fine rule #DEDCD1. White is for focused reading surfaces.
Use system sans-serif for controls and Georgia for editorial display headings.
Tabular figures for nutritional values. No remote font dependency.
Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Corner radius 8–20px, not uniformly rounded.

## Mobile
Bottom navigation, 48px minimum controls, safe-area padding, one primary action
per screen. Camera capture is a guided front/back sequence. Large legible values,
short paragraphs, inline validation, honest loading states and useful recovery.
Show incomplete scans as incomplete; never turn missing values into a confident
rating. Health information and product photos must stay scoped to the account.

## Accessibility and interaction
Visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, labels, reduced-motion support,
screen-reader status announcements, error messages beside the affected action.
No forced onboarding modal. Empty states explain the next action. Offline screens
explain which functions require connectivity. Full and half ratings use numbers.
